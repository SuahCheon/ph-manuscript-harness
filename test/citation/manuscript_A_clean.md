# A Neuro-Symbolic System for AEFI Surveillance (example manuscript)

## Introduction

Adverse events following immunization (AEFI) require structured causality
assessment. The WHO AEFI causality classification provides the global standard
for this process [GREY-LIT-WHO]. Case definitions follow the Brighton
Collaboration framework, which standardizes AEFI reporting across surveillance
systems [GREY-LIT-WHO].

Prior neuro-symbolic approaches to clinical text have shown that combining
neural extraction with symbolic rules improves traceability [PMID-VERIFIED].
National pharmacovigilance guidance from the Korea Disease Control and
Prevention Agency informs the local reporting workflow [GREY-LIT-KDCA].

## Methods

We adopted a purposive cohort as a stress-test rather than a representative
sample. The reproducibility analysis reflects algorithmic consistency, which we
distinguish from clinical validity throughout [PMID-VERIFIED].
